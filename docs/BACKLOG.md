# Prioritized product backlog

This file describes intended features, NOT implemented ones. See STATUS.md for truth.

## P0 — First paid pilot

1. Identity, signup, roles, business onboarding, tenant isolation and audit logging.
2. Web widget, single inbox, conversation assignment, notes and human handoff.
3. One AI agent, structured FAQ/catalog knowledge, constrained answers and safe escalation.
4. Customer profile, conversation history and basic sales pipeline.
5. Services, staff availability, time zones and conflict-free appointments.
6. Trigger/condition/action automation, retry/idempotency and quotas.
7. KPI dashboard, AI-usage budgets, plans, billing and trial lifecycle.
8. Data retention, consent handling, export/deletion, backups, monitoring and security tests.

## P1 — Growth based on pilots

WhatsApp/Instagram/e-mail via approved APIs, Calendar sync, RAG with citations, visual workflows, lead scoring, reporting, campaigns, prepayments, quality evaluations and integrations.

## P2 — Enterprise

Voice, branches, SSO/SAML, external CRMs, deep analytics, multi-agent and enterprise policies.

## Commercial validation

Pick a specific appointment-heavy segment. Interview 10–20 local businesses, demo a complete intake-to-booking loop, seek 2–3 paid pilots, and reprioritize from verified demand. Do not implement all P1/P2 before paid validation.
